description: Verify and translate current ChatGPT/OpenAI updates into useful business actions for Glam Hustle Hub.
---

Use this skill for recent ChatGPT/OpenAI updates, release changes, new features, removed features, plan changes, or rollout questions.

1. Verify current information before answering. Prefer official OpenAI sources and dated documentation.
2. Record the date or time period of the information.
3. Separate verified facts from interpretation.
4. Explain: what changed, what it means for Glam, whether it affects current products/workflows, whether it saves time, whether it creates a business opportunity, and whether it is worth teaching.
5. Use one of these labels when useful: USE THIS, WATCH THIS, TEACH THIS, BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY, LOW PRIORITY, NOT RELEVANT.
6. Never turn a rumor, example, or remembered detail into a current fact.
7. If verification fails, say that clearly instead of filling the gap.
